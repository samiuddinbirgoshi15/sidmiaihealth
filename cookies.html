Cookie Policy

Effective Date: June 2024. At SidmiAI Health, we believe in complete transparency regarding how we use cookies and tracking technologies.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit our digital platform. They help us ensure basic platform functionality, remember your professional preferences, and analyze site performance to improve user experience.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential for core platform operation, user authentication, zero-trust session management, and HIPAA-compliant security routing. These cannot be disabled.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Used anonymously to analyze platform traffic, page navigation patterns, and system latency.
  • Functional Cookies: Allow the platform to remember choices you make (such as UI theme preferences and clinician dashboard configurations).

3. Third-Party Services

We do not sell your personal data or tracking information to third-party advertisers. We utilize secure enterprise infrastructure providers (such as Formspree for lead capture) that may set temporary functional cookies necessary for secure form submission.

4. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You have full control over your cookie settings. You can modify your browser settings at any time to block or delete cookies. Please note that disabling strictly necessary security cookies may impact your ability to access authenticated clinical areas of the platform.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our Cookie Policy or privacy practices, please contact our Data Protection Officer at:
